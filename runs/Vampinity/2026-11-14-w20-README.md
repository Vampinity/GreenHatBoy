# W-20 on my console, 14 Nov 2026 (runs 1469 to 1472)

Four starts of waveydude's W-20 test, staged for him with the controls. Logs as they came off the console, packed with
`tools/pack-run.py`. The only changes are the redactions listed under "redacted" in each run.json: the console user
number, the profile id in the save path, the save's name, my home folder and the other titles (names and ids) in config.json. The
timestamps and numbers are unchanged.

- Build T184 (T181 plus ports, their switches off; eboot sha16 8de0ba545e1105dd, 01.219.028 Nov 14 00:47). The batch
  is called `w20-pack-in-place-t181` because the plan was written for T181. The eboot that ran is T184.
- Play settings v3.9, handheld, 1x, base PS5 on 12.40.
- W = v3.9 + `EDEN_GUEST_SWAP_PACK_IN_PLACE=2`, V = v3.9 alone. Order W V W V. The env.txt files differ only in that line
  and the script name.
- Route (`script.txt`): a save in the field, walked by minimap steering to the gate, then the load into the next area,
  then about 300 s in the area after the field if the load is survived.

## Files per run

`eden_log.txt.gz` (app clock, with the 5 s fps lines and the present lines), `stderr.log.gz` (EDEN_TEST script markers,
FAULT_WAIT, MEM_CENSUS, swap, pack and RADV memory lines), `heap.log.gz`, `env.txt`, `script.txt`, `config.json`,
`report.txt`, `result.json`, `boot-trace.txt`, `run.json`. In 1472 also the crash handler's files `crash-20261114-023138*`.
Its stderr copy has 3360 lines against 3352 in stderr.log, so use that one for the end.

## Two clocks

- The EDEN_TEST lines in stderr.log (`at=`) are on the script clock. It starts at the `EDEN_GUEST_START` line in
  eden_log.txt.
- eden_log.txt's `[ seconds]` and FAULT_WAIT `t_ms` are on the app clock. In FAULT_WAIT end lines `t_ms` is the time the
  wait started, not the time it ended.
- App time = script time + the GUEST_START offset below. I worked out the app times in the table this way; there is no
  app-clock line at the wait_met itself.
- The `t_s` in RADV_PS5_MEMORY lines is the driver's own count. I have not tied it to the app clock.
