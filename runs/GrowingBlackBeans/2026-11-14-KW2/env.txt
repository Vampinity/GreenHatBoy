# runKW2 — the recorded walk, second try (runKW1 got a screenshot, not a video) for the scripted Kokiri route. t134-gbb9 (01.219.925), no new build.
# Same settings as runGS1 (counts only: GPFIFO + submission stats), plus three test-harness switches:
#   EDEN_TEST_RECORD=1        what you press goes to logs/recorded-input.txt, in script format
#   EDEN_TEST_DISCARD_SAVE=1  the game's autosaves go to a throwaway copy, so your house save stays the
#                             newest entry, which is what the scripted runs press Continue on
#   EDEN_GBB_LOG_SESSION=1    our dumps go to their own logs/s<stamp>-<pid>/ folder (first time on)
#
# BEFORE YOU START
#   1. Your start save is the usual one, in Link's house. Back it up once anyway.
#   2. PS5 capture: press Create, then pick "Start new recording" from the menu (NOT the screenshot),
#      BEFORE you press Continue. A recording timer shows on screen while it runs. Stop it after stop D.
#      This video is what the steering points come from: the minimap must be on screen while you walk.
#
# THE ROUTE — the benchmark route and the four stops, as in runGS1 (C at the rock, see STOPS.md):
#   At each stop: tap the targeting button, leave the stick alone, stand 60 s.
#   Walk the way you always do. Smooth and unhurried beats fast: the script will copy your line.
#   Don't fall off the cliff path (if you do, restart the whole run, recording included).
#   Stop A — out of Link's house, hopped down, facing down the main path.
#   Stop B — the back of the forest, facing the Kokiri blocking the path.
#   Stop C — after the ivy wall, where the path before the cliff ends at the large rock on the right,
#            looking out over the whole cliff. NOT at the edge (the camera pans down there).
#   Stop D — the shop sign, facing it.
#
# AFTER: copy the logs folder as usual, and the video file (USB stick, from the Media Gallery).
