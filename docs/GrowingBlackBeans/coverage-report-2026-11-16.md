# Coverage report: what the 1,332 packed runs can tell us (16 Nov 2026)

Phase 1 of the roadmap ends when every archived run has a scored row and this report is published. Every
run now has a row: [`tools/stk/stk_score.py`](../../tools/stk/) (scorer 6) over `runs/` at `d7f8172`, all
1,332 folders, no errors. This report counts what those rows hold.

## The headline

- **789 runs carry 7 of the 10 numbers.** 188 carry 5, 133 carry 4, and 222 carry 2 or fewer.
- **Three numbers are empty in every run — hitches, flashes and game speed.** Nothing in any build logged
  them. They need instruments, not readers.
- **The archive can't be pooled.** Firmware is in the log for 60 runs and the shader store for 15, so
  almost no two runs can be shown to share a setup. It is a record of what was measured, not a control.

are Vampinity's bench runs almost entirely. Hand-played runs end without saying how.

## The three kinds of gap

The dossier named three: missing (never instrumented), unread (in the logs, nothing reads it), unshared
(taken on one console, never packed).

**Missing — needs an instrument.**
- **Hitches**: no run has the flight recorder or a hitch trace on.
- **Flashes**: the in-emulator counter is off everywhere and no 60 fps capture is packed.
- **Game speed**: not instrumented in any build.

**Unread — in the logs, now partly read.**
- **Memory**: 97% once three readers are chained — the driver line (1,070 runs), the pool line (132), the
  census (91). The census and the driver line disagree on a run's minimum (the driver samples less often),
  so the row records which one it used.
- **Frame rate before 8 Nov**: 354 runs have no 5 s fps lines (the line didn't exist yet). 317 of them log
  a present counter every 600 frames, which gives a coarse rate; in 61 runs there are enough samples to
  use it. Nothing reads it yet.
- **Survival in hand-played runs** (411): no end line and no crash file. Unknown, and recorded as unknown
  — not as "no crash".

**Unshared — taken, not packed.**
- Capture video and the bench's recordings, frame grabs and picture checks (the flash counter's input).
- Our 14 runs from 16 Nov (the control starts), until the packer is settled.
- waveydude's runs on his machine (19 are packed; his scorer indexed 129).

## Conditions: why the archive can't be pooled

By date, the lines that make runs comparable arrived late: 5 s fps lines from 8 Nov, `sdk_version` from
12 Nov, the shader store line from 14 Nov. Everything before then can be described but not matched.

## Routes, and the out-of-memory count

- **Route results**: 810 completed, 18 ran to the end with an unconfirmed last stretch (the control
  routes), 22 failed, 189 didn't reach their end, 293 had no script.
- **Out-of-memory lines**: 51 runs have any. 26 of them sit exactly at the log's cap of 64, so those 26
  are floors, not counts.

## What it means

1. **The archive answers "was this ever measured?", not "is this better?"** Comparisons need the control
   starts and the run contract, which record every condition from now on.
2. **Phase 6 is three instruments**: a hitch trace on control runs, a flash count (capture or counter),
   and a game-speed line. No reader will fill those columns.
3. **One cheap reader is left**: a coarse frame rate from the present counter for 61 early runs. Worth it
   only if those runs are needed.
4. **Packing is the gap we can close**: our control starts and the captures.

*Method.* `python3 tools/stk/stk_index.py --roots runs` (scorer 6: waveydude's scorer with the run-contract

"unknown" is not counted. Full rows can be regenerated by anyone with the same command.
