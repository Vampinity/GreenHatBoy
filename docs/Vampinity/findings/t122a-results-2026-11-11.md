## Short answer

- **Speed (A):** T122 22.72 / 22.46 fps standing. No loss against T120 or T125. The gap to T120 (+0.30) is about
  the size of T122's own gap between two identical starts (0.27). So: level.
- **Forest exit (B, G):** crossed **6 of 6**, no crash, all ended by the script. Frames checked in every run.
- **Memory (B, C):** it gets close to empty just before the black. The one run with the 2 s census (the trial)
  reached **16 MiB** largest free block 2.7 s before the black. Three of the five gate runs logged a RADV ask
  that found no single free 16 MiB range. Nothing was refused, nothing failed.
- **Arms (C):** the pipeline memory gate (128, 256) never made a build wait. Burst 300 cut the area clean-up by
  about half and left less free memory after the change. None is worth offering the owner.

  followed (controls: another 54-61 MiB). Over the whole run it deleted 1241 MiB against 2149-2401 MiB in the
  other five; the clean-up after the save load alone was 865 MiB against 1780-1975 MiB, with 381 GPU-written

## G. Verdict

On T122 with the owner's v2.6-fast settings the forest exit is **not** a place where the game dies here: **crossed
6 of 6 without a crash** (plus 35 s in the next area each). Memory gets close to empty: down to **16 MiB** largest
free block 2-3 s before the black in the one run that measured every 2 s, and three of five other runs hit a
moment with no free 16 MiB range just before the black or in the village. It recovers to 260-830 MiB within 20 s.
None of the three arms is worth offering: the gate never engaged, and burst 300 cleaned up less.
