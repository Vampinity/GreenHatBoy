# Faster CPU emulation on T129 (11 Nov 2026)

> **Found:** Faster CPU emulation is neutral on T129 (within 0.3 fps, no crashes); GrowingBlackBeans' 33 fps comes from playing in handheld mode and where he stands, not from this setting.
>
> **So:** Neither recommend nor warn against it; compare handheld against docked and his exact settings file on the same spot instead.

"Faster CPU emulation" is the menu setting `unsafe_cpu`. In the code it switches the CPU translator to its
inexact mode (`cpu_accuracy = Unsafe`: shortcuts on floating-point maths, the sums that use decimal numbers).
Read-only analysis of two batches on build T129 (01.219.028, 11 Nov 18:01), base PS5 on firmware 12.40, docked, full
resolution, fast settings (one frame ahead) plus the usage tracker. Nothing here touched the PS5, the queue or a worktree.

## Verdict

**Neutral. Do not recommend it, and there is no reason to warn against it.**
- Standing outside the house over the village: off 24.1 and 24.4 fps, on 24.1, 23.8 and 24.0. On is 0.3 fps
  lower on average (about 1%). Two runs with the same setting already differ by 0.25-0.3 fps, so the gap is no
  bigger than the run-to-run spread. No gain.
- Forest exit: 28.8, 28.7 fps on against 28.8 off. All three runs sit inside the spread of the other T129 exit controls.
- No crash, no device lost, no VK_ERROR line in 5 runs with it on. Flashes unchanged.
- GrowingBlackBeans' 33 fps does not come from this setting. In his own logs he had it **off** in 112 of 126 runs. His
  standing fps is 32.7 with it off (median of 46 runs) and 31.3 with it on (median of 14 runs, other builds). His one
  clean A/B on the same build and the same settings file: off 31.85, on 32.12. That is one run each, so it shows nothing.
- His gap has other causes: **where he stands** (inside the house, a light view), **handheld mode**, and possibly
  **13 settings lines we lack**. See section 4.

**CPU-side numbers.** Does the setting change anything at all?
- Guest cores (the emulated Switch CPU cores): idle calls per 5 s on the busiest game core are 17,143 and 17,286 off,
  and 16,989, 16,796 and 17,282 on. Other cores are within 3%. The game's CPU cores do not get visibly more idle time.
- The game's own frame chain (main thread → worker pools) is not logged in these runs, because the profiler was off.
  **Not seen.**
- JIT (the translator that turns Switch code into PS5 code): 64 MiB held in four runs and 80 MiB in u2. 28-29 memory
  lines per run in both settings. No difference.
- GPU threads: on runs wait slightly longer at the present (18.4-19.4 ms against 16.7-17.7 ms) and run a frame ahead
  less often (309-334 against 330-373 of 600). GPU busy per 5 s is 3,244-3,289 ms on and 3,363-3,421 ms off.
  This holds in all 3 on runs against both off runs. It is still a small shift with 2 controls, and fps does not move.
  My reading [I]: the game side may finish its frame slightly sooner and then waits longer on the back GPU thread,
  which stays the limit here. A faster CPU cannot show up as fps while that thread decides the frame.

## 4. Why our village gives 22-24 and his gives 33

His files: `runs/GrowingBlackBeans/` in the project repo clone (126 runs with logs, 7-11 Nov). All on a base PS5 on
firmware 13.60. His route: Continue, Link's house, out into the village, a walking loop back to the water. His index
counts "standing" as 10-60 s after the first frame of 300 ms or more.

**1. He stands somewhere else (most likely the main cause).** In his standing windows (about 76-140 s) the
**fence wait is 0.0 ms per frame**. Nothing is waiting on the GPU threads, so the game's own CPU work is the limit.
Texture churn is almost nil: 6 to 244 images created per 600 frames. His back GPU thread is idle 27-31% of every
second. Right after his standing ends (about 140 s), a scene-load burst appears in his log (`EDEN_AREA_EVICT burst 2`,
126-152 new images). Fence wait jumps to 18-25 ms and fps falls to 17-23. That is the walk out of the house into the
village. So his "standing" is **inside the house** [I: strong, from the log; no video to confirm]. Our standing spot
is **outside the door, looking over the village**: fence wait 17-19 ms per frame, with the back thread as the limit.
His village windows where the GPU threads are the limit (heavy, fence ≥5 ms) run **20-23 fps**, not faster than our
24. Our own light views reach his number: Saria's greeting scene in c1 ran 33.3-36.3 fps at fence 0.0 (130-141 s),
and the fps-gap note has 33-37 after the cut.
- *Cheapest test on our console:* a copy of the stand script that stops after Continue (WAIT hearts, then stand 60 s
  inside the house, no door), on our fast file. Expect about 32-33. If it shows that, the "gap" is a different view,
  not a slower console.

**2. Console mode: he plays handheld.** Every one of his 126 launch lines says `Console mode: Handheld`. Ours say
`Docked`. Resolution 1x, bilinear, 1080p, 60 Hz match. His logs have 1920x1080 draw targets next to 2560x1440. Ours
have only 2560x1440, so in handheld the game draws part of its frame at about half the pixels. This matters in heavy
views (village, overlook) and hardly at all inside the house. The fps-gap note measured +10-20% from handheld.
- *Cheapest test:* one menu change (console mode handheld) on our village standing route with the same live-phase
  layout. Two runs each, interleaved with docked. If it gains, it is an fps option for players, not our target
  (docked full resolution stays the goal).

**3. His settings file (R120a, his newest) against our fast file, line by line.**
- *He has, we lack (13):* `EDEN_UP_OOT_FAST_LOOKUP`, `EDEN_UP_OOT_POLL_BACKOFF`, `EDEN_UP_OOT_NATIVE_ZSTD` (three
  game-side CPU patches, port 0447), `EDEN_UP_FRITCHII_WIDEAFF`, `EDEN_UP_FRITCHII_GRHAND`, `EDEN_GBB_NAN_GUARD`,
  `EDEN_GBB_POSITION_INVARIANT`, `EDEN_NX2_MASK_COMPONENTS`, `EDEN_NX2_MASK_UNWRITTEN`,
  `EDEN_NX2_TRANSCODE_THREADS=1`, `EDEN_GBB_DESTROY_UNMAP_ALL` (replaced by our 0578), plus `EDEN_NX2_SPLIT_WAITS` and
  `EDEN_PERF_REPORT` (logging, about 3% cost).
- *Different values:* `EDEN_GUEST_SWAP_AHEAD_MIB` 2048 (his) / 12288 (ours), `EDEN_GUEST_SWAP_COLD_S` 90 / 30,
  `EDEN_NX2_TC_KEEP_ASYNC` 120 / 300.
- *We have, he lacks:* `EDEN_UP_WIDE_COMPUTE_SSBOS`, `EDEN_UP_PIPELINE_LIST_KEEP`, `EDEN_UP_HEAP_WAIT_MS=2000`, the
  usage tracker, `EDEN_NX2_FETCH_COUNT`, `EDEN_NX2_JUNK_TRACE`, test and live-switch lines.
- The three game-side CPU patches are the ones that could help in exactly his light view, where the game CPU is the
  limit. The ledger has them as untested on our side.
- *Cheapest test:* his exact R120a env.txt plus our test lines, interleaved with our fast file, standing both inside
  the house and outside the door. Two runs each.

**4. Firmware 13.60 against 12.40.** I cannot test this on our console. The only difference that shows in the logs
is the swap-ahead size (T130 now picks it by firmware). Where both consoles are held up by the GPU threads, his are
not faster (20-23 against 24), so firmware is an unlikely cause of the gap.

His Performance line otherwise matches ours: block list off, async shaders off, fast GPU on, unsafe DMA off, reactive
flushing off, skip invalidation off.

Not compared: draws or ring words per frame at the same view. His logs have ring words (split waits on): about 3.9
million per second standing, 3.3-3.6 million walking. Ours do not, because split waits were off in these runs.

## 5. Verdict and next steps

Neutral on T129: no fps change standing or at the exit, no picture change, no crash in 5 runs of up to 291 s. Leave
it off in our fast file and in the release text. It is fine if players turn it on. Revisit it only when a light,
game-CPU-limited view is part of a test.

1. House-standing test (Continue, stand 60 s inside) on our fast file: checks reason 1. It is the cheapest and
   explains the most.
2. Handheld against docked at the village standing spot, 2+2 interleaved: checks reason 2.
3. His R120a settings file against our fast file, inside and outside the house, 2+2: checks reason 3.
4. If Faster CPU is tested again: run it inside the house (the only place it can help), with
   `EDEN_UP_AHEAD_GUARD=3` really in env.txt, and longer than 325 s.

**Wrong or weaker than stated.**
1. **"He stands inside the house" is contradicted by his own logs.** The big area load before his standing window
   (the 646 ms frame at 66 s in R120a) creates 619-620 new images (`EDEN_AREA_EVICT burst 1`) in 83 of his runs.
   In our c1 the same 619-image load is at our door (frame 3125, about 90 s, between the frame with "A Open" at the
   door and the village fly-over). The shared index even names that moment `door_s`, and his "standing" is 10-60 s
   after it. So his 33 fps window is **after he walks out of the door, in the village**, not in the house.
   What still differs: right after our door our save plays the fly-over, the place-name card and a greeting
   scene (15-21 fps, then 33-36), and we stand from about 146 s. His log shows no heavy fly-over, so his save may
   skip it, and his exact camera spot is unknown (no video). So the view may differ somewhat, but it is not a
   light indoor view. His later burst 2 (100-152 images, about 130 s) is a further move on his walk, not the door.
2. **The house-standing test premise does not fit our own logs.** While our house shows on screen (frames 19-20 of
   c1, about 75-88 s), the 5 s lines read 59.6-59.7 fps with 0.0 ms fence. Our house is not a 33 fps view.
   "Expect about 32-33 inside the house" is not supported; that test would not explain his number.
3. **Handheld is the most likely main cause, not the second.** His composited frame is `crop 1920x1080`, ours
   `crop 2560x1440` (one line each, R120a against c1). In handheld the whole game frame is drawn at 1080p, about
   56% of our pixels, not "part of its frame". That fits his village window at 0.0 ms fence (the GPU threads keep
   up) against ours at 17-19 ms (they decide the frame). Counter-point to test, not assume: his later walking windows
   where the GPU threads do hold him up run 20-23 fps, about what our exit walk gives (20.3-20.6) docked, but those
   are different views and routes, so they prove nothing either way.
4. **The firmware argument is weak for the same reason.** It compares his walking windows with our standing spot.
   Firmware 13.60 against 12.40 stays untested; it is not ruled out by the logs.
5. "His newest settings file (R120a)" is not his newest: A0-A3, E1, P3 and P5 came 30-75 min later the same morning.
   A3 = R120a plus `EDEN_UP_AHEAD_GUARD`, `EDEN_UP_AHEAD_ALWAYS`, `EDEN_GBB_GUARD_EARLY`, `EDEN_NX2_READBACK_WAIT`
   (and it ran slower, 28.0 against 29.0 for the whole run). The 13-line list against ours is still right.
6. Naming: "Saria's greeting scene" (section 4) names a character; use "a greeting scene" in anything Vampinity reads.
