## Short answer

- **Gate: T126 is not slower with every switch off.** Two counted starts: 22.83 and 22.06 fps (phase means, card.md),
  mean 22.44, against T122's 22.72 / 22.46 (mean 22.59). By the 5 s lines: 23.44 / 22.75 against T122's 23.26 / 22.94,
  both means 23.10. The difference (-0.15 or 0.00) is inside what two identical starts differ by. The spread between
  T126's own two starts (0.77) is wider than usual: one dip in control2 (phase 2, 21.25 fps).
- **Pause menu: the fix does what it is for.** With the area budget on, the six menu visits deleted **1.5-1.7 GiB**
  instead of **7.1 GiB**. Closing the menu made **0 or 290-470** new images instead of **880-1055**. The worst frame at a
  close fell from **350-466 ms** to **157-313 ms**. No fps cost at the overlook. Picture intact in every frame I looked at.
- **Not fixed:** the lowest largest free block was *not* better (fix 181-201 MiB, controls 255-393). Images still
  re-made within 600 frames: about 1,700 per run (the reviewer expected under 300).
- **The verify run (v1) did not test anything.** Its env had `EDEN_UP_AREA_BUDGET_VERIFY=1` but not
  `EDEN_UP_AREA_BUDGET=1`. It printed no area-budget line. It is in effect a second control.
- No crash, no device lost, no refused allocation in any run.

## D. The fix's own lines

10 s lines `EDEN_AREA_BUDGET 10s: …` summed over the run (stderr.log):

| | b1 | b2 |
|---|---|---|
| Windows opened (incl. the load) | 13 | 15 |
| Bursts vetoed as returns (`return:` lines) | 11 | 14 |
| Deleted (MiB) | 2,441 | 2,460 |
| Budget-capped passes | 638 | 753 |
| Re-made within 600 frames (images / MiB) | **1,697 / 1,203** | **1,746 / 1,253** |
| Refusal fallbacks | 0 (every line) | 0 |
| Verify: old extra / bursts | 0 / 0 (verify off) | 0 / 0 |

- **Menu opens:** every open window ended `budgeted, budget left 0 MiB` (b1 L577, L665, L883, L1169, L1431, L1678,
  L1842). The budget stopped the walk each time: that is the "kept" part, about 0.8-1.4 GiB of field per open.
- **Closes:** the first close of both runs was vetoed outright (b1 `return: 135 of 141 new images were deleted here
  within 600 frames; no window`, L776). Later closes still opened a window (290-470 new, fewer than 100 of them
  returns once part of the field had gone), and ended with budget left 365-908 MiB (b1 L1026 815, L1322 739, L1504 446,
  L1804 685, L2018 365). So those closes deleted what was old enough, not what the budget allowed.
- **Walk bursts:** at the B-cycle walks, bursts of 100-106 with 2-20 returns were vetoed (b2 `return: 2 of 100` L1303,
  `13 of 106` L1346, `6 of 104` L1527). In the controls the same walks opened a window and dropped the whole field.
  These are bursts on the 100 mark, not real loads (inferred from the times).
- **Churn is still high:** about 1,700 images / 1.2 GiB per run came back within 600 frames, against the review's
  "under about 300". The budget eats field images once the old leftovers are gone, as the review warned. It is less
  than the controls (where every close remade about 960), but not near zero.
- **Verify mode: no counts.** v1 lacked `EDEN_UP_AREA_BUDGET=1`. VERIFY only acts with the switch on (README "with the
  switch on"). v1 has 0 `EDEN_AREA_BUDGET` lines (grep over stderr.log, eden_log.txt, heap.log). So there is no
  difference count to trust or reject. Its env line (plan.json, run1125 env.txt) has only
  `EDEN_UP_AREA_BUDGET_VERIFY=1`.

## H. Verdict in plain words

- **Does it stop the pause menu from emptying the area? Yes, mostly.** Fix vs control:
  - deleted 1.5-1.7 GiB against 7.1 GiB;
  - each open deleted 24-225 MiB against 0.8-1.6 GiB;
  - closing made 0 or 290-470 new images against 880-1,055;
  - the worst frame at a close halved (157-313 ms against 351-466).
- **Cost:** none seen in fps (overlook 14.9-15.0 in all four). Off cost at the village gate: none seen.
- **Picture:** intact. The flashes are the same kind as in the controls.
- **Not better:** the lowest largest free block (181-258 against 255-393), and churn of about 1,700 re-made images
  per run.
- **Unproven:**
  - verify mode (v1 ran without the switch);
  - the door load and the forest exit (other batches, still running: not judged here);
  - the door walk out and back within 20 s, the case the review names as the risk;
  - a long play run;
  - the refusal fallback, which never fired.
- **Ready to offer the owner for his own play? Not on this evidence alone.** It does its job on the menu and costs
  nothing visible. But the risk the review names is a real load taken for a return and then running out of memory,
  and this route cannot show that. Offer it once the door, forest-exit and door-walk gates pass with no `return:` line
  before an out-of-memory line.
