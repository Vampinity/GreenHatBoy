### Passage lag

The few-fps lag was not reproduced. In all three valid runs the walk into the passage ran at 28-34 fps in the 5-second windows. The worst single frame there was 45-110 ms, with no freeze. The slow part of the route is the village before the passage. Every run, fixes on or off, sat at 15-18 fps for about 30 s there. During that stretch the emulator was mainly decoding and swapping textures. It made 3000-4700 new images per 600 frames, ran 50-300 compressed-texture decodes per 5 s on the graphics chip, and on average each frame waited 17-21 ms for the graphics chip to finish. Very few shaders were built (1-9 per 10 s), and swap barely moved (about 2 ms on average per page). The lag Vampinity saw likely needs what this short walk does not have: memory already worn down by 8+ minutes of play, or a first visit to new places (here the shaders were kept from earlier sessions). The ~530-540 ms frame in every run is the moment the world first appears after loading, before Link can move.
