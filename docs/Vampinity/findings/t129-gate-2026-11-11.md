**Where a cost could come from, if it is real (INFERRED, from the placement notes and reviews, not measured):**

- T126's 0557 (a switch read on every register method, about 2 million a second) is still in; the one-line fix in
  the heavy-gate note is not applied.

## 8. Verdict

T129 with everything off is **level with T120** (-0.20, inside the noise, passes the plan's rule) and **at the edge
of the noise below T122 and T126**: -0.50 and -0.35 on counted starts, best guess -0.3 to -0.4 fps (about 1.5%),
honest range about 0 to -0.8. Not "costs nothing", not "proven slower". On speed alone it can still go to testers
as a dev build (memory note: a build goes out once it passes the console check), but **no speed claim against
T122/T126** until the back-to-back gate runs.
